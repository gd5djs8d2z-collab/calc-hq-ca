Northwest Territories Tax Brackets 2026

The Northwest Territories taxes income at four rates, from 5.90% to 14.05%, with an $18,198 basic personal amount that reaches further than Ottawa's. Nothing else bends its rates, no surtax, no premium and no reduction that shrinks with income, so there is less to explain here than for most provinces. These are the 2026 territorial and federal brackets, and what each band pays once both are combined.

The territory's top rate starts at $172,346, ahead of Ottawa's 29% bracket, which begins at $181,440. That leaves a band where a dollar costs 40.05%, between the 38.20% below it and the 43.34% above. All four territorial thresholds rose with indexation this January.

What the table doesn't include

Most people who live in the Northwest Territories for at least six months in a row can claim the northern residents deduction on line 25500 of the federal return. It lowers taxable income, not the rates, so to use the table below, look up your income after that deduction rather than before it. The engine's figures here don't apply it, because its value depends on where you live and on travel benefits from an employer.

On a salary the rates run slightly lower until $85,000, where contributions stop: CPP and EI premiums earn a credit, and enhanced CPP is a deduction. From there on the two agree, with each threshold shifted $1,127 up the pay scale.

A $100,000 salary in Yellowknife

On $100,000 of pay, the engine charges $13,302 of federal tax and $5,724 of territorial tax, 19.0% of the salary between them, before any northern residents deduction. The next dollar costs 29.10%, the federal 20.5% plus the territory's 8.6%.

Common questions

What are the Northwest Territories tax brackets for 2026?

5.90% on taxable income up to $53,003, 8.60% up to $106,009, 12.20% up to $172,346, and 14.05% above that, with federal tax charged on top.

What is the top combined tax rate in the Northwest Territories?

47.05% on taxable income over $258,482, where the federal 33% rate applies on top of the territory's 14.05%.

Does the northern residents deduction change my tax bracket?

It can. The deduction reduces taxable income, so it can move part of your income down into a lower bracket, but it does not change the rates themselves. Look up your taxable income after the deduction to find your marginal rate.
