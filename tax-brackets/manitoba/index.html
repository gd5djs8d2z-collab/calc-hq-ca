Manitoba Tax Brackets 2026

Manitoba's tax brackets and basic personal amount are exactly what they were in 2024. The province stopped indexing them from the 2025 tax year, and at the same time began taking the $15,780 personal amount away from anyone with net income between $200,000 and $400,000. That makes Manitoba's steepest combined rate, 51.25%, a middle band rather than the top one. What follows is Ottawa's 2026 schedule, Manitoba's, and the cost of the next dollar in each band.

Three Manitoba rates, frozen in place

One taxable income is taxed twice, once by each government, and every rate reaches only the income inside its band.

The $47,000 and $100,000 thresholds date from 2024. Manitoba's 2025 budget ended their indexation, along with the basic personal amount's, and the 2026 budget made no change to personal income tax. Federal thresholds keep rising each January, so a Manitoba raise that merely keeps up with prices pushes a little more income into the 12.75% and 17.4% bands every year the freeze lasts.

The personal amount that disappears

Above $200,000 of net income, Manitoba's basic personal amount shrinks by 7.89% of every extra dollar of income, and by $400,000 it is gone. Since the amount is worth 10.8% of itself as a credit, losing it costs $426 at $250,000 of net income and the full $1,704 from $400,000 up.

Spread across the band, that adds 0.852% to the Manitoba rate. Between $258,482 and $400,000, where Ottawa's top rate also applies, the combined figure reaches 51.25%; past $400,000, with nothing left to phase out, it drops back to 50.40%.

A credit at the bottom, too

At the other end of the scale Manitoba has a family tax benefit, open to any filer whose income is low enough. It starts at $2,065, shrinks by 9% of income, and is valued at 10.8% like the personal amount, so it is worth up to $223 and runs out at $22,944. With it, Manitoba tax starts once taxable income passes $16,371 rather than at $15,780, and the Manitoba rate on the stretch it covers is 11.77%. A spouse, dependants, age 65 or a disability add larger amounts; only the basic amount goes into these calculations.

Common questions

What are the Manitoba tax brackets for 2026?

10.8% on taxable income up to $47,000, 12.75% up to $100,000, and 17.4% above that. These are the 2024 thresholds; Manitoba stopped indexing them from 2025.

Does Manitoba's basic personal amount phase out?

It does, starting with 2025. The $15,780 amount shrinks evenly as net income rises from $200,000 to $400,000 and is zero above that, which costs up to $1,704 of Manitoba tax.

Why is the Manitoba marginal rate lower above $400,000?

Because the basic personal amount has finished phasing out. Between $258,482 and $400,000 each dollar also removes part of that credit, for a combined 51.25%; above $400,000 there is nothing left to remove and the rate is 50.40%.
