Nova Scotia Tax Brackets 2026

On a $65,000 salary, Nova Scotia's income tax comes to $6,348 and Ottawa's to $6,307. Outside Quebec, no other province takes more than the federal government at that income. The reasons are an $11,932 basic personal amount, the smallest in Canada, and a second bracket that starts at $30,995. Both 2026 schedules follow, each band shown with its combined rate.

Nova Scotia began indexing its brackets, its basic personal amount and four other credits with the 2025 tax year, and indexed them again this January. Its 14.95% rate now starts at $30,995, about half the point where Ottawa's second rate begins, and its 21% top rate from $157,124 makes a combined 54.00% on the highest incomes.

The smallest reduction, and it isn't indexed

Nova Scotia's low-income tax reduction is $300 for a single person aged 19 or older, reduced by 5% of net income over $15,000, so it is gone at $21,000. Those amounts are written into the Income Tax Act, and they are not on the list that indexation covers, so while the brackets rise each year the reduction stays where it is.

Together with the basic personal amount, it means no Nova Scotia tax until taxable income passes $15,219, still below the $16,452 at which federal tax begins. For a little over a thousand dollars of income, Nova Scotia is the only government taxing it. The reduction rises to $600 for a couple and adds $165 for each dependent child; the calculators here give only the single-filer amount.

Common questions

What are the Nova Scotia tax brackets for 2026?

8.79% up to $30,995, 14.95% up to $61,991, 16.67% up to $97,417, 17.5% up to $157,124, and 21% over that, with federal tax on top.

What is the Nova Scotia low-income tax reduction?

A reduction of up to $300 in Nova Scotia tax for a single person, less 5% of net income above $15,000. It ends at $21,000. Couples and parents can claim more, and the amounts are not indexed to inflation.

Are Nova Scotia tax brackets indexed?

Yes, since the 2025 tax year. The brackets, the basic personal amount and four other credits rise each January with the consumer price index. The low-income tax reduction is not indexed.
