OAS and GIS Payment Dates (2026)

Old Age Security has one monthly payment date, and the Guaranteed Income Supplement, the Allowance and the Allowance for the Survivor ride on it: Service Canada lists all four under a single OAS schedule, so GIS has no dates of its own. Payments arrive in the final days of each month, December excepted. Four of them a year, in January, April, July and October, bring a new amount.

Outside December the 2026 dates run from the 25th, in February and September, to the 29th, in July. December's payment, on the 22nd, is the earliest of the year by three days.

The quarterly change

OAS and GIS are re-indexed four times a year. When the Consumer Price Index rises, the payment rises with the January, April, July and October deposits; when it falls, the payment stays where it is. Service Canada compares the latest three months of the index with the last three-month period that triggered an increase, so a quarter can pass with no change at all.

That makes those four deposits the ones to compare against the previous month. GIS amounts also depend on your income and are reset each July from the previous year's return, so July can move for two reasons at once.

Getting the money

Direct deposit is the fastest way to be paid. Cheques go in the mail over the final three business days of the month, so they land after the date shown here, and Service Canada asks anyone missing a payment to give it five to ten business days, then call. GIS comes on the same day as the OAS pension.

How much? The OAS calculator covers the pension and the recovery tax, and the GIS calculator estimates the supplement from your income and marital status.

Common questions

When is OAS paid in 2026?

January 28, February 25, March 27, April 28, May 27, June 26, July 29, August 27, September 25, October 28, November 26 and December 22, 2026.

Is GIS paid on a different day from OAS?

No. Service Canada pays the Guaranteed Income Supplement, the Allowance and the Allowance for the Survivor on the Old Age Security dates. There is no separate GIS calendar.

When do OAS payments increase?

In January, April, July and October, if the Consumer Price Index has gone up. If it goes down, the amount does not decrease.
